Who we are

We exist to help communities grow with dignity.

LivGro Vision Foundation works towards creating meaningful social impact by supporting health, education, happiness and prosperity.

Our work is rooted in service. We believe that sustainable change happens when people are empowered with opportunities, care and the confidence to shape their own future.
